Privacy policyImmersion Dashboard · last updated 3 November 2026

This policy covers Immersion Dashboard (the web app at mattias.github.io/immersion-dashboard, the Windows app and the Android app 旅の記録) and the Immersion Web browser extension. In short: your data stays on your devices and in your own Google Drive. We run no server, and we never receive, see, sell or share your data.

What the apps read

  • Study data on your computer, from the sources you turn on: Anki reviews, GameSentenceMiner, e-book and manga readers, immersion-mpv, Mokuro and a sleep file. It is read where it already is and never changed.
  • Garmin Connect (Windows app, optional): you sign in on Garmin's own page, and that page hands the app your nightly sleep hours and score. The app never sees your Garmin password.
  • Immersion Web (browser extension, optional): while Japanese video plays on Netflix, YouTube or Twitch, it records the date, site, title, channel or show, episode, seconds watched and the number of Japanese subtitle characters shown. It does not record other browsing.
  • Android app (optional): if you grant notification access, it sees which media is playing in the YouTube, YouTube Music, Netflix and Twitch apps, and records the date, app, title, channel and seconds played. It does not read the content of your notifications.

All of this is stored on your device, in the app's own storage.

Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional and only needed for Sync between devices. The apps ask for one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which gives access to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only this app can use. They cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive.

  • What is accessed: the app's own files in that folder: immersion-dashboard.json (what the dashboard read and its settings), immersion-web.json (from the extension) and immersion-phone.json (from the Android app).
  • How it is used: only to show your own statistics on your own devices. The files go straight between your device and Google; they never pass through any server of ours.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, sold or transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens are kept on your device only. The files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is not collected

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no cookies of our own, and no accounts. The web app loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the web app's sign-in loads Google's sign-in script; Google's own privacy policy applies to those.

Deleting your data

  • In the dashboard, Settings → Your data → Erase everything removes all data from that device. Uninstalling the apps or the extension does the same.
  • To delete the synced files, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Immersion Dashboard → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • To withdraw the app's access, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source repository. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
