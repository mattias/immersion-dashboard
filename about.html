旅の記録 · Immersion DashboardA private dashboard for Japanese learners

Immersion Dashboard gathers what you read, play, watch and review in Japanese from the apps you already use, and shows it by day, week, month and year: time, characters, streaks, a year calendar, the titles you spent time on, your Anki reviews and, if you like, your sleep.

Open the dashboard Windows and Android apps Source code

No server, no account

Everything the dashboard reads stays on your own devices. If you turn on Sync between devices, a copy goes to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your phone can show what your computer read. The dashboard cannot see anything else in your Drive.

Why it asks for Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional. It is only used to keep one file, immersion-dashboard.json, in the app folder of your Google Drive (the permission drive.appdata), and to read the files that the Immersion Web browser extension and the Android app put in the same folder. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Sources

Anki, GameSentenceMiner, ttu Ebook Reader, yatsu, Hoshi Reader, Mokuro, immersion-mpv, Netflix, YouTube and Twitch (through Immersion Web or the Android app), Garmin Connect and a sleep file. Every source is optional.
